Lecture 6: Greedy Algorithms & Activity Selection

Key Concepts:
1. Greedy Choice Property: A globally optimal solution can be arrived at by making locally optimal (greedy) choices without looking ahead or recalculating past decisions.
2. Activity Selection Problem: Select the maximum number of mutually compatible activities in a single shared resource. Greedy strategy: sort activities by finish time, always choose the earliest finishing compatible activity. Runs in O(n log n) time and is proven optimal.
3. Optimal Substructure: Required for both DP and Greedy algorithms. The optimal solution contains optimal solutions to subproblems.
4. Knapsack Problems Comparison:
   - Fractional Knapsack: Can take fractions of items. Greedy strategy based on highest value-per-weight ratio yields optimal solution in O(n log n).
   - 0-1 Knapsack: Items must be taken whole. Greedy strategy fails. Requires Dynamic Programming with running time O(n W).
